Useful details, before we start.

Campaign availability and operating requirements vary by offer. These answers outline the usual discussion points before a partnership begins.

Which offer verticals do you focus on?

Campaign interests include personal finance and loan offers, auto insurance, home improvement (including windows, roofing, gutters, bathrooms and solar), and selected consumer promotions. Availability depends on current partner offers.

Which traffic sources can you use?

Potential channels include native, display, social, email, pop and push traffic. A source is used only where it is permitted by the specific advertiser or network, approved for the campaign and appropriate for the target audience.

Which geographies do you target?

Geography is campaign-specific. US-focused offers are part of the business context, but the permitted GEO, audience eligibility and any state-level restrictions must be confirmed for each offer.

How do you track campaigns?

Tracking may include campaign IDs, source parameters, Sub-IDs, click IDs where supported, conversion postbacks and reporting through platforms such as BeMob or RedTrack. The exact setup depends on partner integrations and campaign requirements.

Can you guarantee lead volume or results?

No fixed volume, conversion rate, payout or outcome is promised on this site. Forecasts and targets should be discussed using campaign-specific data and terms.

How can a network or advertiser get in touch?

Use the Partners page to share the vertical, GEO, payout model, conversion definition, traffic rules, caps, validation criteria and technical tracking requirements. Avoid sending sensitive consumer information in an initial inquiry.

Are all listed networks currently active relationships?

Relationships, platform access and offer availability can change. The Networks page lists names from the working ecosystem; please contact us to confirm the current status and suitable offers.

Do you accept every traffic source for every offer?

No. The advertiser or network’s written terms take priority. Incentivized, email, pop, push, social and other traffic types must be explicitly permitted for the offer before use.
